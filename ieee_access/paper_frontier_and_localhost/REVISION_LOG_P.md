# Revision log P: the restructured draft, screened and adopted (2026-10-09)

The author had an external model revise the paper against a reader's critique (the draft felt overworked: the argument restarted in every section, too much terminology arrived before it was useful, compressed slogans competed with spatial metaphors, the most interesting findings sat inside classification detail, and the framework sounded defended rather than earned). The external model worked from the latest text alone, without the data or the earlier audits, so the author asked for it to be screened for factual mistakes and misrepresentations before adoption. This round adopts that draft as the final text after the screen.

## What the draft changed

The section order now follows the critique's progression: a deployment keeps changing while its model stays fixed (I); a change needs evidence before it counts as an improvement (II, the loop); the corpus (III); what local adaptation actually closes (IV); what the feedback allows a gate to know (V); when a local improvement is shared (VI); how adaptation can undermine its own evidence (VII); what governed sharing would require (VIII); how the research lines fit together, with the earlier-papers hypothesis and the related surveys moved here (IX); open problems and conclusion (X). The eight tables, the four figures, and the citation key set carried over unchanged.

## The screen (`scratchpad/factcheck_P.md`)

One Opus reader checked every factual sentence of the draft against the released table, the committed body, the supplementary, and the dated correction notes. Every number was right and every table verbatim; the settled recodes (SkillForge and SkillRL in [None × None], OptiMAS ungated, DGM's validity check, AlphaEvolve inside C1, Letta's ungated push, VACE not a crossing, EDDOps, Future AGI excluded) were carried correctly. What was wrong, and is now fixed:

- **Nine mechanism misstatements.** The gate labels "hide" the calibration distinction (they do not record it); calibration "recorded separately" (there is no such column); NanoResearch, AutoManual, and ShareMem "not all" estimating a loss change (none does); SkillRL "rewrites" (it adds unconditionally); "the corpus documents" the failures (the literature does); SkillOpt-Lite "filters reflections" (it keeps attributes common across rollouts); SkillChain's person deciding whether a reflection "enters the bank" (the person decides which refined skills are deployed); one system, SIA, turned into "systems"; "on one substrate" for "on at least one".
- **Three mis-attached citations.** The AGENTS.md specification attached to an adoption fraction; Ong et al. attached to a re-scoped finding (restored to "cannot be told from trial and error by score alone", twice); SIA used for a plural claim.
- **Systematic softening**, reverted: "nearly every" had become "common", "widespread", and "most"; "never" had become "often"; "only in curation" had become "primarily"; "unmeasured elsewhere" had become "most do not"; "no system has both" had become "largely separate"; "each line / nobody's" had become "generally / seldom"; the categorical grid predictions were hedged; the production-viable pairing was dropped; "none of the registries generates a candidate" was narrowed to EDDOps; receiving-side evaluation "establishes" (it tests; it is necessary, not sufficient); "in practice they are rare" was replaced by a disclaimer the authors never made.
- **Four omissions that changed meaning**, restored in the draft's own order: the three-paper argument's conclusion ("not a workaround for an inadequate model but the architecture the error structure calls for: a frontier model that generalizes, a local scaffold that specializes, and reliability that comes from governing the specialization") in IX-A and the three-sentence ending in the conclusion; the prompt-optimization lineage "is not a deployed loop" and ACE "never promotes" its playbook; no system rolls back a promotion that crossed an organization because none makes one; the research promoters' pool is as far as any travels, with no access control or receiving-side gate.
- **Four unsupported additions** replaced or marked: the relaxation findings list (now S2's); "safely distributes" (admission control); "both inform the governance dimensions"; derivation tracking as a sixth architecture part (now an inference from VaG).
- **Two ground-truth corrections the screen found in passing**, applied to the draft: SkillChain's person gates deployment, not bank admission (the released table and S4 had it right; the earlier body did not); the skill-repository edits were committed under named human accounts and human-merged, most with an AI co-author trailer (the earlier "human-authored" overstated it).
