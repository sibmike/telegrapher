## The two-loop design space {#sec:design}

Index each system by the gate on each half of the loop and the field collapses into two occupied cells and a half (Fig. REF(fig:design)).

```{=latex}
\begin{figure}[!t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.9\columnwidth]{fig3_design_space_ieee}
\caption{The two-loop design space. Rows give the Loop 1 gate and columns the Loop 2 gate, so a cell [Loop 1 $\times$ Loop 2] such as [Auto $\times$ Human] pairs an algorithmic local gate with a human promotion gate. Two cells hold every system that passes the composite audit; a third holds the governance-first platforms, which have the gated second loop but no inner loop; the human-authored cells are occupied only by registries and curation workflows that have no session-signal loop. No populated system adds governed cross-tenant promotion.}
\label{fig:design}
\end{figure}
```

[Auto × Auto] holds the research full-auto systems, machine-evaluated on both loops, and Letta's Context Repositories, which push shared memory across agents automatically with git lineage and no evaluation gate; the push is unconditional, an algorithmic promotion with no threshold, and the composite audit admits it on lineage, not on a gate. [Auto × Human], the production-viable cell, holds SkillForge, CASCADE, Sierra Agent OS 2.0, and Cognition Devin: Loop 1 fires at machine speed under an algorithmic criterion, and Loop 2 waits for a person. [None × Auto] holds the governance-first platforms, which gate promotion on evaluation but generate no candidates from failures; they pass only the Loop-2 half of the audit and enter the matrix as contrast entries (Supplementary Material S4).

Seven systems promote across users, clients, teams, or repositories with no versioning or lineage on what they promote, and fail the audit on that alone. If the audit asked for a promotion mechanism and nothing more, six of them would enter, all in [Auto × Auto] except Spotify's production loop, which would land in [Auto × Human]; none would open a new cell. The seventh, Future AGI's prompt lifecycle, rests on a vendor blog alone, which the tier rule does not count, and is not admitted under any rule (Supplementary Material S2).

The other cells, where occupied at all, hold only systems that fail the composite audit, and in them the Loop 1 label records who supplies the candidates rather than who gates them. [Human × Auto] is empty: nobody authors candidates by hand and promotes them automatically, because automating the promotion of hand-written candidates buys little. [Human × Human] holds MCP registries and a community hub [@agenticcommunity2026mcpgateway; @microsoft2026apicentermcp; @truefoundry2026mcpgateway; @jiang2026buildrix], where a person registers an artifact and a person approves its use; the field study of five public skill repositories finds the same shape in practice, with every substantive edit human-merged and one deprecation in 254 edits [@shen2026whomaintains]. [Human × None] is the curation cell: an agent drafts, a person fact-checks, and the result is frozen [@nguyen2025verificagent]. These are single-loop by construction, and the supplementary matrix records the convention (Supplementary Material S4).

The occupancy bears out the three consequences of Section REF(sec:signal). Every [Auto × Auto] system whose gate estimates the loss change per edit runs on an exact signal or a simulated outcome, and those that work on delayed signals prune proposals instead. [Auto × Human] holds only systems whose signal is an observed outcome or a judgment; no exact-signal system sits there. And no system in the matrix calibrates a judge that is itself under edit.

Two further patterns cut across the cells. The newer systems batch evidence before acceptance, where earlier ones accepted an edit on one evaluation and absorbed estimator noise through the per-edit margin alone: a paired per-item test commits an edit only when its wins are reliable [@wang2026sagegate]; a merge is accepted only when it fixes at least as many held-out items as it breaks [@nie2026evolvenet]; three heterogeneous critics must all pass [@shang2026vag]; mini-batch failure signals feed validation-based selection [@park2026autosaddler]. Requiring agreement among independent proposals and averaging over them are different operations that reduce error at different rates (Appendix REF(app:gates)). Multi-layer hierarchies remain rare: AlphaEvolve is the only system that combines a cascade evaluator with a diversity archive, and the other systems that keep archives use them for lineage, not for staged evaluation.
