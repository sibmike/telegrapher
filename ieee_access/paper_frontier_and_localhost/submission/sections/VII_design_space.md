## The two-loop design space {#sec:design}

Indexing systems by the gate on each loop gives a 3×3 matrix, and its occupancy is lopsided (Fig. REF(fig:design)).

```{=latex}
\begin{figure}[!t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.9\columnwidth]{fig3_design_space_ieee}
\caption{The two-loop design space. Rows give the Loop 1 gate and columns the Loop 2 gate, so a cell [Loop 1 $\times$ Loop 2] such as [Auto $\times$ Human] pairs an algorithmic local gate with a human promotion gate. Two cells hold every system that passes the composite audit; a third holds the governance-first platforms, which have the gated second loop but no inner loop; the human-authored cells are occupied only by registries and curation workflows that have no session-signal loop. No populated system adds governed cross-tenant promotion.}
\label{fig:design}
\end{figure}
```

[Auto × Auto] holds the research full-auto systems, machine-evaluated on both loops, and Letta's Context Repositories, which push shared memory across agents automatically, with git lineage and no evaluation gate. That push is unconditional, an algorithmic promotion with no evaluation threshold, so it sits in the Auto column of Loop 2; the composite audit admits it on lineage, not on a gate. [Auto × Human], the production-viable cell, holds SkillForge, CASCADE, Sierra Agent OS 2.0, and Cognition Devin: Loop 1 fires at machine speed under an algorithmic criterion, and Loop 2 waits for a person. [None × Auto] holds the governance-first platforms, which gate promotion on evaluation but generate no candidates from failures; they pass only the Loop-2 half of the audit and enter the matrix as contrast entries (Supplementary Material S4).

Seven systems promote across users, clients, teams, or repositories without versioning or lineage. Six of them would enter the matrix if any of versioning, lineage, or rollback counted as Loop-2 governance, landing in [Auto × Auto] except Spotify's production loop, which lands in [Auto × Human]. The seventh, Future AGI's prompt lifecycle, rests on a vendor blog alone (tier T5), which the tier rule does not count, and is not admitted under any rule. None opens a new cell (Supplementary Material S2).

The other cells, where occupied at all, hold only systems that fail the composite audit. [Human × Auto] is empty: nobody authors candidates by hand and promotes them automatically, because automating the promotion of hand-written candidates buys little. [Human × Human] holds MCP registries and a community hub [@agenticcommunity2026mcpgateway; @microsoft2026apicentermcp; @truefoundry2026mcpgateway; @jiang2026buildrix], where a person registers an artifact and a person approves its use. The field study of five public skill repositories finds the same shape in practice, with every substantive edit human-merged and one deprecation in 254 edits [@shen2026whomaintains]. [Human × None] is the curation cell. An agent drafts, a person fact-checks, and the result is frozen [@nguyen2025verificagent]. These are single-loop by construction. The Loop 1 label in these cells, as in [None × Auto], records who supplies each candidate: a person who registers an artifact for a specific use (Human) or an engineer who authors a version at a desk for no particular failure (None); the distinction is one of convention, and the supplementary matrix records it (Supplementary Material S4).

Read by signal class, the occupancy bears out the first two predictions of Section REF(sec:signal). Every [Auto × Auto] system whose gate estimates the loss change per edit runs on an exact signal or a simulated outcome, and those that work on delayed signals prune proposals instead. [Auto × Human] holds only systems whose signal is an observed outcome or a judgment; no exact-signal system sits there. The third is the scaffold-side finding that section already records, that three systems report judge calibration and none calibrates a judge under edit, and no system in the matrix changes it.

Evidence is batched before acceptance, whereas earlier systems accepted an edit on one evaluation and absorbed estimator noise through the per-edit margin alone. A paired per-item test commits an edit only when its wins are reliable [@wang2026sagegate]; a merge is accepted only when it fixes at least as many held-out items as it breaks [@nie2026evolvenet]; three heterogeneous critics must all pass [@shang2026vag]; mini-batch failure signals feed validation-based selection [@park2026autosaddler]. Requiring agreement among independent proposals and averaging over them are different operations, and they reduce error at different rates (Appendix REF(app:gates)).

Multi-layer hierarchies remain rare. AlphaEvolve is the only system that combines a cascade evaluator with a diversity archive; the other systems that keep archives use them for lineage, not for staged evaluation.
