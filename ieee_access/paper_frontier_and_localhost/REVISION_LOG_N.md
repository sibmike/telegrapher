## What the passes did, in substance

Most style changes split a sentence that carried two ideas or three stacked clauses into two sentences at a semicolon or a comma-and (the gate paragraph in VI, the AGENTS.md studies, revocation and rollback, SkillOpt and SkillOpt-Lite, the thesis sentence and the two-decisions sentence in VII, the grid-reading sentence, the evidence-tier and audit sentences in V, the Ratchet and evaluation-fragility sentences in X, problems 1, 3, and 5 in XI). A few moved a late verb forward ("A targeted intervention budget then covers the residual; that budget grows..."; "The feedback a deployment has, rather than its domain, decides which of them it should expect to meet"), removed a nominalization ("report the same for harness optimizers"), or restored one name per concept ("the half most systems skip"; "each scope's loss"; "the failure log"; "observable-outcome class"). The texture changes vary openings and clause shape where runs had formed (a fronted "Because" in II-A, "On the governance part, two neighbors come close", "Drift dominates:", "SkillRL is the second:", "Turned inward, the ledger ... supply the responses"), break one parallel run of example sentences in IV, and turn two colon-joined sentences into periods where three colons sat in a row in VII's opener.

What the agents saw and left alone, by their own notes: deliberate cross-section echoes ("an improvement in what the check measures"), a scope condition that must stay in front of its claim in II-B, the terse abstract clauses, and the long sentences that still read at speed.
