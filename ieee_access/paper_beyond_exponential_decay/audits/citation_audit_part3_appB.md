## The "on our reading / on our calculation" sentences

- **τ-bench (l.474):** the arithmetic is correct (0.612^8 = 0.0197) and so is the Jensen argument. As written, though, the conclusion rests on an upper bound ("below 25%"), and the numbers are not scoped to τ-retail. See H1.
- **Mixture of exponentials (l.476):** sound. The mixture lies above the single exponential with the same initial hazard at every n > 0, and its hazard decreases. The extension to agent curves needs the assumption in L16.
- **Ross–Bagnell tight example (l.443):** sound for the supplement's example, which I verified line by line: the learner errs only in s0 and then pays cost T. It is overgeneral as "the example", because Kääriäinen's tight example has uniform per-step error. See M2.
- **Kalai–Vempala floor on e_key (l.454):** overreaches (average versus per-decision, arbitrary versus rare, unprompted generation, calibration premise). See M6.
- **SelfCheckGPT (l.454):** sound. An error repeated in every sample scores as consistent, and voting returns it.
- **UID (l.463):** sound. "Over half the tokens ... almost no entropy" matches main-text l.217 (Qwen3-8B, entropy below 0.01).
